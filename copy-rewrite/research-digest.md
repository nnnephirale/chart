# Research digest: mainstream astrology meanings

Compiled 2026-10-02 from beginner-friendly sources, at least two per topic, paraphrased. Astro.com blocked automated reading, so none of its pages are used here. Use this file for facts, and write the copy in your own words.

## 1. A natal chart
- A map of the sky at the minute and place you were born, seen from Earth.
- Planets are the who (kinds of energy), signs are the how (style), houses are the where (life area), and aspects are how the planets get along.
- The birth time decides the houses, the rising sign and the MC.
- Sources: cafeastrology.com/articles/how-to-read-natal-chart-report.html · chani.com/blogs/astrological-key-terms · en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natal_astrology

## 2. Bodies (core line + keywords)
- **Sun:** core self and life energy. identity, purpose, vitality.
- **Moon:** feelings, and what makes you feel safe. emotions, comfort, nurturing, moods.
- **Mercury:** how you think, talk and learn. mind, words, curiosity.
- **Venus:** love, beauty, what you value. attraction, harmony, pleasure, money and values.
- **Mars:** drive and action. energy, courage, anger, desire.
- **Jupiter:** growth and luck. expansion, optimism, generosity, wisdom.
- **Saturn:** rules, limits, hard work. discipline, responsibility, time, maturity.
- **Uranus:** sudden change, originality. rebellion, freedom, invention.
- **Neptune:** dreams, imagination. intuition, compassion, fog or illusion, art.
- **Pluto:** deep change, power. transformation, intensity, hidden things.
- **Generational planets.** Uranus spends about 7 years in a sign, Neptune and Pluto 12 to 30+ years, so their signs describe a whole generation. They become personal when they touch the Sun, Moon, inner planets or angles.
- **North Node:** a math point where the Moon's path crosses the Sun's. It marks the direction you're growing toward. The South Node, opposite it, is your comfort zone.
- **Chiron:** a small icy body found in 1977, orbiting between Saturn and Uranus. Called the "wounded healer": a sore spot that becomes wisdom you can use to help others.
- **Ascendant (rising):** the sign rising in the east at birth, at the 9 o'clock point of the wheel. It's your first impression or "front door", and it changes sign about every 2 hours.
- **Midheaven (MC):** the top of the chart and the start of the 10th house. Career, reputation, public direction.
- Sources: astrostyle.com/astrology/planets/ · cafeastrology.com/natal/neptune-pluto-signs.html · cafeastrology.com/natal/uranus-signs.html · cafeastrology.com/nodesofthemoon.html · cafeastrology.com/chiron.html · astrostyle.com/astrology/chiron/ · cafeastrology.com/risingsignsascendant.html · cafeastrology.com/midheaven.html

## 4. Houses
1. Self: how you come across, first impressions
2. Money and values: income, belongings, talents, self-worth, security
3. Communication: talking, school, siblings, neighbours, short trips
4. Home and family: roots, feeling safe
5. Fun and creativity: hobbies, play, romance, kids
6. Daily routines and health: work habits, chores, pets, helping
7. Partners: close one-to-one bonds
8. Deep bonds and shared things: trust, shared money, big inner changes
9. Big ideas and travel: beliefs, higher learning, long trips
10. Career and reputation: public image, ambitions
11. Friends and groups: teams, causes, hopes
12. Hidden and inner life: rest, solitude, dreams, behind the scenes

- **House groups:** angular houses (1, 4, 7, 10) are the action houses, strongest and most visible. Succedent houses (2, 5, 8, 11) build and keep. Cadent houses (3, 6, 9, 12) learn and adjust. Use this modern framing, not "weak".
- **Empty houses** are normal and not bad. To read one, look at the sign on its cusp and that sign's ruler.
- Sources: astrostyle.com/astrology/12-zodiac-houses/ · chani.com/blogs/the-12-houses-in-astrology · cafeastrology.com/houses.html

## 5. Aspects
- **Conjunction (0°):** the planets blend and act as one team. Strong, sometimes a blind spot. Call it neutral.
- **Sextile (60°):** friendly. An opportunity that works when you use it.
- **Square (90°):** friction. Uncomfortable, but it pushes you to act and grow.
- **Trine (120°):** easy flow, a natural talent. Easy to take for granted.
- **Opposition (180°):** a tug-of-war. You need balance, often learned through other people.
- **Orb:** how close to exact the angle is. Tighter means stronger. There's no single standard, roughly 8 to 10° for the Sun and Moon and 5 to 7° for the others.
- **Stellium:** 3 or more planets in one sign or house. That area gets loud.
- **Singleton:** the only planet in an element, mode or hemisphere. Astrologers treat it as a focus point: it carries that whole element for the chart and often stands out. Lu's Venus is the only planet in a fire sign. Sources: kerykeion.net/content/learn-astrology/foundation-singleton-planets · thetarotlady.com/star-school-lesson-15-element-strength-and-weakness/
- **Out-of-sign aspects** count by degree, not by sign. Lu's Moon, Mars and Saturn in early Pisces square Pluto in late Scorpio even though both are water signs, and Venus trine Pluto is also out of sign. Don't write "these water signs flow, yet they square". Just say the angle makes friction.
- Sources: cafeastrology.com/articles/aspectsinastrology.html · astrostyle.com/aspects/ · astrologyking.com/aspects/ · astrostyle.com/astrology/stellium/

## 6. Retrograde
- **Astronomy:** an illusion of viewpoint, like passing a slower car. Earth overtakes the outer planets. Mercury and Venus lap Earth. The Sun and Moon are never retrograde.
- **How often each planet is retrograde:** Mercury about 19% of the time, Venus 7%, Mars 9%, Jupiter 30%, Saturn 37%, Uranus/Neptune/Pluto 41 to 44%. A retrograde slow planet at birth is common, not rare or bad.
- **Natal meaning:** the planet's energy turns inward. It works privately, in your own way and on your own timetable, and gets revisited. Different, not damaged.
- **Nodes:** they drift backwards through the zodiac, one lap every 18.6 years. The mean node is always retrograde. The true node is mostly retrograde with brief direct stretches.
- Sources: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apparent_retrograde_motion · planetary.org/articles/what-does-mercury-in-retrograde-actually-mean · cafeastrology.com/natal/retrogradesinthenatalchart.html · en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lunar_node
