## 1. How houses work

### What a house is
- The chart wheel is cut into 12 slices. Planets say *what* kind of energy, signs say *how* it behaves, houses say *where* in life it plays out. [S]
- Houses depend on the exact birth time and place, not just the date. That's why two people born on the same day can have very different houses. [S]
- AstroTwins' image: the houses are the "sets" of the movie of your life and the planets are the actors. [S]
- Counter-clockwise from the 1st, the houses trace a path from the private self (1st) out to society and the collective (12th). [S]
- **Personal vs interpersonal houses** (AstroTwins): houses 1 to 6 are your private, immediate world (self, money, siblings, home, fun, routines); houses 7 to 12 are relationships, career, society, spirit. Lots of planets in 1 to 6 can mean staying loyal to home and old friends; lots in 7 to 12 can lean career and adult relationships. [S] Lu splits the difference: six planets in houses 1 and 2, four in houses 10 and 12 (plus the North Node in the 10th). [D]
- Liz Greene (via Wikipedia) reads each house psychologically: the 1st as the mask, the 4th as the unconscious family foundation, the 7th as a mirror for what we project onto others, the 8th as where power, control and vulnerability surface, the 12th as where buried fears and desires get faced in solitude. [S]

### Placidus, and what it means for this chart
- Placidus is the most widely used house system in modern Western astrology. It gives houses of different sizes, and the further from the equator you're born, the more uneven they get. [S]
- Near the equator, Placidus houses come out almost equal. [S] Lu was born at 1°16'N, so her houses run from about 27.6° (5th and 11th) to 32.6° (2nd and 8th). [D]
- No intercepted signs: every one of the 12 signs sits on a cusp, so every house has its own ruler. [D] (Interceptions are a high-latitude problem. [S])
- Opposite cusps share the same degree (1st/7th both 1°54', 2nd/8th both 2°29', and so on), which is how every quadrant system works. [D]

### Angular, succedent, cadent (modern framing)
- **Angular (1, 4, 7, 10):** the four corners of the chart: Ascendant, IC, Descendant, Midheaven. AstroTwins compare them to compass points and to cardinal signs: they start things and set direction. Planets here act most visibly. William Lilly wrote that planets in angles show their effects most forcefully. [S]
- **Succedent (2, 5, 8, 11):** they follow the angles and are compared to fixed signs. They build, keep, and make things real through repetition; they're about what belongs to you and takes root. [S]
- **Cadent (3, 6, 9, 12):** the last house of each quadrant, compared to mutable signs. They learn, adjust, process, and prepare the next step. The old Greek word means "falling", which is why older texts call them weak; use the modern "learning and adjusting" framing. [S]
- **Lu's spread:** angular = Mercury (1st), Jupiter and Pluto (10th); succedent = Sun, Moon, Mars, Saturn, Venus (2nd); cadent = Uranus and Neptune (12th). Astrolog's house-mode power is Angular 40.34%, Succedent 40.16%, Cadent 19.50%. [D, from the report]

### The six axes (each house and its opposite)
Opposite houses work as a pair, like two ends of a seesaw. [S]
- **1st and 7th: me and you.** How I meet the world, and the person who meets me face to face. The 7th is often where we find, or project, the qualities we don't own in ourselves. [S]
- **2nd and 8th: mine and ours.** What I earn, own and value on my own, against what's shared, merged, owed, inherited or lost. Self-worth on one side, trust and intimacy on the other. One summary: the 2nd asks "what is mine?", the 8th asks what happens when mine becomes ours, or has to be let go. Healthy balance means building something solid and still letting it change. [S, partly W]
- **3rd and 9th: facts and meaning.** Everyday learning, talk and the local world, against big beliefs, higher learning and far travel. In the 3rd we collect knowledge; in the 9th we make sense of it. [S]
- **4th and 10th: roots and reputation.** The private base (home, family, where you come from) against the public peak (career, status, what you're known for). Cafe Astrology: the 4th is what you've inherited, the 10th is where you're heading. Alice Sparkly Kat (via search summary): the IC holds what you push down and come from, the MC what pushes on you and what you're trying to prove. [S; Sparkly Kat line W]
- **5th and 11th: my spark and our cause.** Personal joy, play and standing out, against friends, groups, shared hopes, where nobody is the star. [S]
- **6th and 12th: daily care and letting go.** Routines, work, health and service, against rest, retreat, the hidden and the unconscious. [S]

### How to read any house: cusp sign plus ruler
- The **sign on the cusp** (the house's starting edge) gives the house its style. [S]
- The **ruler** is the planet that rules that sign. Cafe Astrology calls it the house's "roving delegate": it carries that house's business to whatever house it sits in, linking the two life areas. Where it sits, its sign and its aspects show how the house's themes actually play out in your life. [S]
- Cafe Astrology adds that a house's ruler shows what we *do* to act out that house; for example, the 10th-house ruler shows how we carve out an identity. [S]
- The Astrology Podcast calls house rulers an ancient technique that modern astrology often skips, and the main way you see how different parts of a chart are connected. Its example list includes double cases such as "ruler of 7th and 8th in 2nd", so one planet can bring two houses' business into one place. [S]
- Each house also has a **natural sign and ruler** (1st Aries/Mars, 2nd Taurus/Venus, ... 12th Pisces/Neptune). Many modern astrologers assume a house and its matching sign share a flavour. [S]

### Empty houses
- Normal and not a bad sign. There are 12 houses and only about 10 main bodies, so nearly everyone has empty houses. [S]
- An empty house still works. It isn't missing or weak; it just isn't a spotlight. Read it through the cusp sign and the ruler's sign, house and aspects. [S]
- elsaelsa's example: her empty 10th is ruled by Saturn and Uranus in her 8th, and she built a career in psychology and astrology (8th-house topics). [S]
- One summary suggests an empty house's themes can run more simply, without the inner complications that planets in the house bring. [W]

### Modern vs traditional rulers
- The ancients only knew the seven visible planets. After Uranus, Neptune and Pluto were discovered, modern astrologers gave them Aquarius, Pisces and Scorpio. Traditional astrology keeps Saturn, Jupiter and Mars. [S]
- Mainstream modern practice often reads both: modern ruler first, traditional as co-ruler. One source says that dropping either one throws away half the sign. [S, W on the "half" wording]
- In Lu's chart this matters a lot: Aquarius (1st), Pisces (2nd) and Scorpio (10th) are all dual-ruled cusps. [D]

### Stellium in a house
- Three or more planets in one house or sign. That life area gets concentrated energy. [S]
- Strength: real depth and skill in that area. Pitfall: lopsided focus, so you may need to bring balance on purpose. [S]
- Read the house first, then let the specific planets (and their sign) tell the story. [S]
- **2nd-house stellium specifically:** focuses a life on earned resources, possessions, values, appetite, talent and self-worth; money, voice, craft and security can shape a life; safety gets measured in what can be held or built. Astrology.com is upbeat (strong at making and managing money, firm sense of self-worth, financial independence) but warns about losing sight of other people's needs. [S; astrology.com is shallow, use lightly]
- A stellium is a point of emphasis, not proof of any outcome. [S]

### Planets on or near a cusp
- William Lilly's "5-degree rule": a planet up to 5° before the next cusp is read as belonging to that next house. [S] It comes from Ptolemy and is debated; it's used most in horary. [S]
- A planet sitting right on a cusp strongly lights up that house. [S]
- Robert Wilkinson (Aquarius Papers): treat cusps as zones; a planet in the zone works in both houses and links them. [S, one source]

### Joys of the planets (traditional, well sourced)
- Each of the seven classical planets "rejoices" in one house where it's most at ease: **Mercury 1st, Moon 3rd, Venus 5th, Mars 6th, Sun 9th, Jupiter 11th, Saturn 12th.** [S]
- The logic: day planets (Sun, Jupiter, Saturn) have their joys above the horizon, night planets (Moon, Venus, Mars) below it, and Mercury, which switches teams, sits on the horizon in the 1st. [S]
- No planet has its joy in the 2nd, 4th, 7th, 8th or 10th. [S]

### Traditional (Hellenistic) house names
- 1st: Hour-Marker / helm; Latin *Vita* (life). [S]
- 2nd: **"Gate of Hades"**, the first house fully below the horizon, the doorway to the underworld half of the chart. Latin *Lucrum* (gain). Vettius Valens tied it to livelihood and giving and receiving. Chani: the Greek word was *bios*, meaning livelihood. [S]
- 3rd: **"Goddess"** (house of the Moon goddess); Manilius called it the Temple of the Moon. Latin *Fratres* (brothers). [S]
- 4th: *Genitor* (parent); also "subterranean" / bottom of the sky (IC = *Imum Coeli*). [S]
- 5th: **"Good Fortune"**. Latin *Nati* (children). [S]
- 6th: **"Bad Fortune"**. Latin *Valetudo* (health). [S]
- 7th: Setting place; Latin *Uxor* (spouse). [S]
- 8th: **"Idle place"**, *Mors* (death). Modern: house of death and rebirth, or "the sex house". Chani calls it the threshold, the "gate of Hades" (note: Hellenistic sources give that name to the 2nd; Chani uses it for the 8th, so the sources disagree). [S]
- 9th: **"God"** (house of the Sun god); *Iter* (journey). [S]
- 10th: Midheaven; *Regnum* (kingdom). [S]
- 11th: **"Good Spirit"** (good daimon); *Benefacta* (support, good deeds). [S]
- 12th: **"Bad Spirit"** (bad daimon); *Carcer* (literally prison; Wikipedia's table translates it as "Rehabilitation"). Modern: **"house of self-undoing"**, house of secrets, hidden enemies. [S]

### Good and difficult houses, and "aversion"
- Traditional astrology treated the 6th, 8th and 12th (and to a lesser degree the 2nd) as difficult, partly because they make no classical aspect to the Ascendant (called *aversion*: they can't "see" the rising degree). The 2nd is called succedent and dark, with no Ptolemaic aspect to the Hour-Marker. [S]
- Modern astrology doesn't call houses good or bad; each has its own job. Wikipedia's modern summary takes the same line. [S]

### 1st house: self, body, first impressions

**Names:** House of Self; *Vita* (life); the Hour-Marker; angular; cusp = Ascendant / rising sign. Mercury's joy. [S]

**Core meaning** [S]
- Where you meet the world: your outer personality, body, looks, gestures, vitality and the vibe you give off before you've said much.
- Your first instinct when something new starts, and how you start things.
- AstroTwins: the house where a spirit settles into a body. Wanting to be seen here is less vanity than checking "am I really here?" They also warn of a survival-mode edge: we're beginners here.
- Steven Forrest (via AstroTwins) and Liz Greene (via Wikipedia) both describe the rising sign as a mask: the face you put on to deal with life while deeper layers stay hidden.
- Cafe Astrology adds the defence mechanisms, early conditioning and much that is automatic, so self-awareness matters here.
- The ruler of the 1st is the **chart ruler**: Chani calls it your main planetary stand-in or avatar.

**Aquarius on the 1st (Aquarius rising)** [S]
- Wants to stand out from the crowd; original, independent, a bit rebellious against the standard way. Comes across friendly but detached and objective. Idealistic, can rise above selfish needs for the good of many. Stubborn when pushed. "Ahead of your time." Cafe names both chart rulers, Uranus and Saturn, and says to look at both.

**Chart ruler(s) and where they sit**
- **Modern ruler Uranus in the 12th** (25°16' Capricorn, conjunct Neptune). [D placement]
  - *Ruler of the 1st in the 12th* (Cafe, modern): shy when young, later learns to handle and mask sensitivity; strong intuition; senses hidden forces behind daily life; always looking for higher meaning and purpose; a private, mysterious aura; capable of being alone but can get lonely or anxious; managing deep sensitivity can be a lifelong task. [S]
  - Kerykeion (summary): selfhood feels elusive and works most truly behind the scenes. [W]
  - Another summary: with the chart ruler in the 12th, you can't simply claim that planet's energy as "yours"; it works through hidden or private channels. [W]
  - [T] 1927 Muchery: hard early life, better ending. Fatalistic; don't use literally.
- **Traditional ruler Saturn in the 2nd** (4°54' Pisces). [D placement]
  - *Ruler of the 1st in the 2nd* (Cafe, modern): a strong value system; security and things that last matter more than to most; can be fixed in routines; wants to put down roots; comfort and security drive much of what you do; gives some Taurus-like traits even without Taurus planets. [S]
  - [T] Muchery: rises by own merit; commercial gains.
- So the self is pulled two ways: hidden and inward (Uranus, 12th) and grounded in values and security (Saturn, 2nd). [D] Saturn rules both the 1st (traditional) and the 12th, and Uranus sits in the 12th, so the 1st and the 12th are tied together by both rulers. [D]

**Planet inside: Mercury 24°08' Aquarius**
- Mercury in the 1st (Cafe): quick, restless, adaptable, easily distracted, nimble; enjoys talking, may talk over people or plan the next line instead of listening; a bit mischievous; meets new things with curiosity and logic before emotion. Cafe says it's strongest within about 10° of the Ascendant: Lu's Mercury is about 22° past it, so the "mercurial look" part is weaker. [S, D distance]
- AstroTwins: acts on the fly, sometimes speaks before thinking, charismatic; turn goals into challenges. [S]
- **Mercury is in its joy here** (the 1st is Mercury's traditional house of joy). [S fact, D application] Rare and worth a line: the only planet in Lu's chart sitting in its joy.
- Mercury also rules the 5th (Gemini) and 8th (Virgo), so both of those houses route into the 1st. [D] See those sections.
- Mercury in Aquarius is ruled by Uranus in the 12th, so the 1st-house mind also answers to the 12th. [D]

### 2nd house: money, belongings, values, self-worth

**Names:** House of Possessions; *Lucrum* (gain); Greek *bios* (livelihood); **"Gate of Hades"** (the first house fully below the horizon, entrance to the underworld half of the chart); succedent; natural sign Taurus, natural ruler Venus. No planet has its joy here. Traditionally "dark" and in aversion to the Ascendant. [S]

**Core meaning** [S]
- What you have, hold, earn and build: money, possessions, income, talents and skills.
- What makes you feel safe and comfortable; Cafe: the bank-balance level at which you feel secure.
- Self-worth and core values: what you cherish most, whether you can rely on yourself, how you use time, money and energy.
- The body and senses: AstroTwins call it the house of the five senses, like a baby discovering it has a body; throat, neck and voice (finding your voice, speaking up for your needs).
- Shadow side (Cafe): greed, overindulgence, over-attachment to things.
- Liz Greene (via Wikipedia): the 2nd is the inner value system, which shapes emotional security as much as money.
- AstroTwins: first "earth house" and the zone of "practical magic"; succedent, so it turns ideas into something real through repeated effort.

**Pisces on the 2nd** [S]
- Cafe: either relaxed and trusting about money or impractical and inclined to look away from it; builds a personal philosophy about material things; generous; trusts that "the universe will provide", and it usually does; watch for disorganisation or waste.
- [D] Pisces is the natural sign of the 12th house, so the "gate of Hades" house wears the sign of endings, dreams and surrender. With four Pisces planets inside, the 2nd reads less like a vault and more like a tide.

**Ruler(s) and where they sit**
- **Modern ruler Neptune in the 12th** (22°47' Capricorn). *Ruler of the 2nd in the 12th* (Cafe, modern): income tied to some kind of service, healing, welfare or helping people who are struggling; can find it hard to judge your own worth, which can affect income; getting an honest read on your talents opens doors; possible quiet or behind-the-scenes financial support. [S] [T] Muchery: struggles to establish your position.
- **Traditional ruler Jupiter in the 10th** (14°29' Scorpio R). *Ruler of the 2nd in the 10th* (Cafe, modern): income comes through your career; daily effort builds a sense of achievement; can point to work in public life or a high-status profession. [S] [T] Muchery: a comfortable position, money that comes quickly.
- [D] The 2nd's own rulers sit *outside* it, so the busiest house in the chart takes its cue from the 12th (modern) or the 10th (traditional). See section 3.

**Planets inside** (Sun 20°01' Pisces, Moon 3°37' Pisces, Mars 2°39' Pisces, Saturn 4°54' Pisces, Venus 2°47' Aries)
- **Sun in the 2nd** (Cafe): pride in what you own, a strong need for security, staying power, hates breaking a promise; warns against confusing who you are with what you have; prove your worth through your values and your loyalty to people and things. Alternate reading: drive aimed at building financial security. [S] AstroTwins: patient, practical, sensible and sensual; likes to build things right the first time so they last. [S]
- **Moon in the 2nd** (Cafe): feelings and money are linked; can swing between generous and frugal; fears debt and needs the bills paid to feel safe; may spend on impulse when upset; holds people tightly; can depend on others' praise until self-worth is built from within; slow, deliberate to show feelings; keeps sentimental things that link to the past. [S] AstroTwins: dependable, "the rock"; relationships as long-term investments; can get too comfortable or stubborn. [S]
- **Mars in the 2nd** (Cafe): hard worker who likes producing useful things; ambitious about money and possessions but impatient or impulsive in spending; possessive of things and people; physical strength and stamina feed self-worth; likes earning things on its own; defends its values fiercely. [S] AstroTwins: industrious and tenacious for security; may need a bigger appetite for risk. [S]
  - Mars sits only 10' past the 2nd cusp. A planet right on a cusp strongly colours that house [S]; a birth time well under a minute later would push the cusp past Mars and put Mars in the 1st. [D, rough estimate]
- **Saturn in the 2nd** (Cafe): a strong need for security; early experiences can leave a fear of loss around money and things; can tie self-worth too closely to achievement; guilt if spending, rigidity if frugal; fear of being taken advantage of can make you miss chances; fear-driven money decisions tend to go badly; it eases with time and experience. [S] AstroTwins: a huge work ethic; set boundaries or burn out. [S]
- **Venus in the 2nd** (Cafe): love shown in tangible ways; strong, well-defined taste; finds quality; falls in love gradually and stays; wants a partner who feels safe; sensual (touch, smell); prefers simple, genuine people. [S] AstroTwins: relaxed is when you're open to love; a slow, romantic courtship. [S] Venus is the natural ruler of the 2nd house, so it's at home by house even though Aries is an "away game" by sign. [S facts, D combination]
  - Venus at 2°47' Aries is 2°16' before the 3rd cusp (5°03' Aries). Under Lilly's 5° rule it would count toward the 3rd house; under a "cuspal zone" reading it bridges the 2nd and 3rd. Astrolog places it in the 2nd. [S rule, D application] Flag this if a card says "five planets in the 2nd".
- **The stellium as a whole:** see section 1. Five bodies here is 36.95% of Astrolog's house power, the top house. [D from report] Note that Moon, Mars and Saturn all sit within 2°25' of the cusp and are conjunct each other (Astrolog's two "Stellium-3" lines group Moon, Mars, Saturn and Mercury). [D]

### 3rd house: talk, learning, siblings, neighbourhood

**Names:** House of Communication; *Fratres* (brothers); **"Goddess"** / Temple of the Moon (Manilius); cadent; the Moon's joy; natural sign Gemini, ruler Mercury. [S]

**Core meaning** [S]
- Everyday communication: talking, texting, writing, gossip, news, small talk.
- Siblings, cousins, classmates, neighbours; the local world, short trips, how you get around.
- Early schooling and how you naturally pick up information; Cafe calls it the "lower" or everyday mind, its key phrase "I think".
- Common sense, curiosity, restlessness, nerves; hands, arms, lungs.
- Liz Greene (via Wikipedia): how early interactions shape mental habits.
- AstroTwins: the first "air house", where we discover there are other people out there; cadent, so it's where we brainstorm and test ideas.

**Aries on the 3rd** [S]
- Cafe: needs movement and stimulation; direct, gets to the point, impatient with hesitation; inventive ideas; wants to be first to know and doesn't like being told what to do; can point to sibling rivalry or being an only child. Cafe says to read Mars for this house.

**Ruler Mars in the 2nd** (2°39' Pisces) [D placement]
- *Ruler of the 3rd in the 2nd* (Cafe, modern): a pleasant, soothing voice; practical and a bit possessive of ideas; thinks about what ideas can *do*; sentimental about childhood and long-lasting childhood friendships; can earn through teaching or communication; learns best by doing or demonstration and remembers what she can use; likes physical books and writing things down; known for common sense. [S] [T] Muchery: good dealings with siblings; profitable trips.
- [D] Odd tension worth noting: the sign (Aries) says fast and blunt, while the ruler's house (2nd) and sign (Pisces) say slow, soft and practical. Mars in Pisces conjunct the Moon and Saturn gives the "fast" Aries mind a cautious, feeling-led delivery.
- Venus may also act here under the 5° rule (see 2nd). [S rule, D application]

### 4th house: home, family, roots, inner foundation

## 3. Patterns worth noticing

### 3.1 The funnel into the 2nd house [D, method S]
Tally of where each house's ruler sits:

| Ruler lands in | Modern rulers | Traditional rulers |
|---|---|---|
| **2nd** | 3rd (Mars), 4th (Venus), 6th (Moon), 7th (Sun), 9th (Venus), 12th (Saturn): **6 houses** | 1st (Saturn), 3rd, 4th, 6th, 7th, 9th, 10th (Mars), 12th: **8 houses** |
| 1st | 5th, 8th (Mercury) | 5th, 8th |
| 10th | 10th (Pluto), 11th (Jupiter) | 2nd (Jupiter), 11th (Jupiter) |
| 12th | 1st (Uranus), 2nd (Neptune) | none |

- Of the seven empty houses (3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 9, 11), **five (3, 4, 6, 7, 9) are ruled from the 2nd**. The 5th goes to the 1st, the 11th to the 10th. Counting the 8th (Chiron only, no classical planet), its ruler goes to the 1st. [D]
- So the brief's examples all check out: Mars (3rd), Venus (4th and 9th), Moon (6th), Sun (7th), Saturn (12th, and traditionally the 1st) are all in the 2nd. Under traditional rulers, Mars adds the 10th too. [D]
- **What astrologers say about this** [S, then D]:
  - Sourced: a ruler carries its house's business to the house it sits in, linking the two (Cafe's "roving delegate"; Astrology Podcast; elsaelsa).
