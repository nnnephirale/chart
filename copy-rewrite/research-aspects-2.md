## Shared background: orbs and out-of-sign aspects

**Orbs (how far from exact an aspect can be and still count)**
- There is no single standard. Wikipedia gives roughly 10° for conjunctions (8° if neither the Sun nor the Moon is involved), 5–10° for squares, trines and oppositions, and 4–5° for sextiles. The Sun and Moon get wider orbs. [S]
- Astrology King prints a "maximum orb" on each aspect page, e.g. 7° for Jupiter conjunct Midheaven. [S]
- Cafe Astrology makes a less obvious point: a very tight aspect tends to work automatically and habitually, like a blind spot that other people notice before you do, while a looser one is something you're more aware of and identify with. [S]
- Astrolog's power scores fall off steeply with orb. In Lu's report the wide aspects score near the bottom: Jupiter trine Saturn 1.65, Mercury conjunct Mars 1.49, Mercury square Jupiter 0.41, Moon conjunct Mercury 0.28, Moon trine Jupiter 0.06, against 27.45 for Saturn square Pluto. [S: source-lu-astrolog.txt]
- **How to write wide orbs [D]:** an aspect at 8°–11° is a background note, a theme that shows up some of the time or needs a trigger. Say "a loose link" or "a softer echo", not "you are…". Several of these wide ones (Moon trine Jupiter, Moon conjunct Mercury, Mercury square Jupiter) would be dropped altogether under tighter orb settings, which is worth knowing if the pipeline ever uses different software.

**Out-of-sign ("dissociate") aspects**
- Definition: the planets are the right number of degrees apart, but their signs don't match the aspect. Example: a conjunction between 29° Capricorn and 0° Aquarius. It happens near sign edges. [S]
- **[DISAGREE] on strength.**
  - Astrolibrary's glossary says dissociation spreads out and weakens the aspect. [S]
  - Skyscript's glossary (seen only in a search snippet; the page blocked fetching) says they're either weaker or they stand for a barrier that has to be overcome before the aspect can work fully. [S, snippet]
  - On an elsaelsa forum, experienced posters say an aspect close to exact by degree is real, but the signs "mute" it: a hard aspect between friendly signs is easier to live with. [S]
  - Elsa P (elsaelsa.com) frames them as a real link with an unusual, "exotic" quality, to be enjoyed and not dismissed. [S]
- **Working rule for the copy [D]:** count them, but describe the mixed flavour honestly. An out-of-sign square between two water signs (Pisces Saturn, Scorpio Pluto) is friction by angle and kinship by element. An out-of-sign conjunction (Aquarius Mercury, Pisces Mars, Pisces Moon) joins two planets that speak different "languages" (air and water). This is consistent with the digest's rule not to write "these water signs flow, yet they square": just say the angle creates friction, and mention the sign kinship as something that softens it, if at all.

Sources: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astrological_aspect · cafeastrology.com/articles/aspectsinastrology.html · astrologyking.com/jupiter-conjunct-midheaven/ · astrolibrary.org/definition/dissociate-aspect/ · skyscript.co.uk/glossary/dissociate-aspect (snippet only) · elsaelsa.com/forum/astrology/out-of-sign-aspects/ · elsaelsa.com/astrology/how-to-interpret-out-of-sign-aspects/

## 1. Sun trine Jupiter · Pisces Sun △ Scorpio Jupiter (R) · 5°31' · 2nd/10th

**Core meaning [S]**
- Optimism, generosity and faith in life and people. People with this aspect seem lucky partly because they expect good things and are generous, so good things come back to them (Cafe).
- Likeable, rarely combative or very competitive, and they keep their promises (Cafe).
- They like travel, foreign places and big ideas; there's a pull toward teaching, philosophy or spirituality (Cafe, Astrology King).
- Strong moral sense, with some impatience for people who break the rules (Cafe).
- **Difficulty:** because life tends to go easily, they may never build the muscles that struggle builds. They can get lazy, feel helpless when real hardship arrives, or slide into excess and over-generosity (Astrology King).

**What the signs add**
- Jupiter is the traditional ruler of Pisces, so this trine links the Sun to its own sign's ruler. In traditional terms Jupiter "disposes" the Sun: wherever Jupiter is in good shape, the Sun is supported. [S for rulership: digest §3; D for the reading]
- Jupiter in Scorpio (Astrostyle): magnetic, all-or-nothing, intense, obsessive focus, interests become obsessions, trust issues, and a placement linked with luck in money, investing and finding backers. Drawn to psychology, investigation and healing. [S]
- Pisces Sun with Scorpio Jupiter, both water [D]: the optimism isn't the loud, cheerful Sagittarius kind. It is faith in feeling, intuition and the hidden side of life, a belief that going deep pays off. Jupiter also sits in mutual reception with Mars (Mars in Pisces, Jupiter in Scorpio, traditional rulers; digest §8), so Jupiter is closely tied into the Pisces group.
- Jupiter retrograde [D, using digest §6]: the growth and faith are worked out privately and in your own way, and the "luck" may feel like an inner trust more than outward breaks.

**What the houses add [D]**
- 2nd (money, values, self-worth) trine 10th (career, reputation). The usual reading of a Sun–Jupiter trine (generosity, faith, good fortune) lands on earning and on public standing. What you value and how you earn tends to support your reputation without much effort.
- The 2nd and 10th are trine houses by position (both "earth" houses in the house–sign analogy: 2 Taurus, 6 Virgo, 10 Capricorn), so the house geometry agrees with the aspect. Some astrologers call 2, 6 and 10 the houses of substance or wealth. [D; the house-triplicity idea is common teaching, but I didn't find a beginner source to cite]
- Astrology sites say Jupiter in the 10th favours a respected public role, with the reputation often building slowly and holding once built (search summaries of kerykeion and Authority Astrology; pages not fully fetched). [S, light]

**In-sign / out-of-sign:** in-sign (Pisces to Scorpio, water trine water). [D]

**Generational:** no. The Sun reaches a trine with Jupiter twice a year for a few weeks. [D]

**Orb:** 5°31' is moderate: well inside every standard trine orb (5–10°). Separating: both the Sun moving forward and Jupiter moving backward are widening it. [D]

## 2. Saturn square Pluto · Pisces Saturn □ Scorpio Pluto (R) · 6°51' · 2nd/10th

**Core meaning [S]**
- **Gifts:** remarkable resilience and a hard, even ruthless, determination to succeed. Recognition through overcoming adversity (Astrology King). Endurance: learning to handle pressure, rebuild after hard chapters, and turn fear into focused mastery (natalecho).
- **Difficulties:** telling healthy persistence from stubbornness. Resisting change out of fear. An armoured, controlled exterior over strong emotional needs. Fear of the unknown that can turn self-defeating, especially around intimacy (Astrology King). Swinging between control and chaos, all-or-nothing thinking, suspicion (natalecho).
- **Psychology:** two drives pull against each other, Saturn's need to keep and protect structure and Pluto's need to tear down what isn't real and rebuild deeper. It is a restless inner tension (natalecho). The growth path is staying flexible in personal life while using the drive in work, and the lesson may take several painful rounds (Astrology King).
- General Saturn–Pluto themes from Cafe's conjunction and sextile pages: resources and power, intense ambitions kept private, resourcefulness, turning bad situations around, problems with authority. [S, by analogy: Cafe has no dedicated square page]

**What the signs add**
- Saturn in Pisces (Astrostyle): a natural healer with strong intuition and compassion, but prone to self-sacrifice, weak boundaries, escapism and fear of abandonment. The lesson is to own your feelings and let others own theirs, and to learn to say no. [S]
- Pluto in Scorpio is at home (modern rulership) and is generational (1983–1995). [S: digest §7]
- Pisces Saturn square Scorpio Pluto [D]: friction by angle between two water signs. The pressure is emotional and private. A plausible reading is Pluto's need for total depth and control pushing against Saturn in Pisces, whose boundaries are porous and whose fear is of dissolving or being abandoned. The water kinship may make it feel less like a fight and more like an undertow.
- Saturn also sits within about 1°–2° of the Moon and Mars, so in practice this square is part of a bigger picture: the Astrolog list also has Mars square Pluto 4°36' and Moon square Pluto 5°34'. Pluto squares the whole early-Pisces trio. [S: source-lu-astrolog.txt; D for the framing]

**What the houses add [D]**
- 2nd (self-worth, money, security) square 10th (career, public role). Saturn in the 2nd: an Authority Astrology summary and natalecho (search summaries only) say self-worth and money are built slowly, often after early messages that worth must be earned, with caution and saving. Pluto in the 10th: power, intensity and reinvention in career. The square suggests that security needs and career ambitions pressure each other: wanting control over your path while fearing the instability that change brings.
- Note the oddity: the 2nd and 10th are trine houses by position (see #1), yet this aspect is a square. The friction is entirely in the degrees, not in the sign or house layout. [D]

**In-sign / out-of-sign:** **out-of-sign.** Pisces and Scorpio are trine signs, but 96°52' apart by degree. See the out-of-sign notes above: the angle counts, and the shared water element may soften it. [D]

**Generational: yes, broadly [S + D].**
- Ephemeris check [D]: the waxing Saturn–Pluto square (first square after the 1982 conjunction) was exact three times: 20 Mar 1993, 9 Oct 1993 and 2 Jan 1994, each with Saturn in late Aquarius and Pluto in late Scorpio (an in-sign fixed square). Saturn entered Pisces for good on 29 Jan 1994. So Lu was born about ten weeks after the last exact hit, with the square separating.
- Anyone born from roughly late 1992 to early 1995 has it within about 8°. Most of those born before 29 Jan 1994 have it in-sign, as a fixed Aquarius–Scorpio square (the exception is Saturn's brief first dip into Pisces, 21 May to 30 Jun 1993). Lu's group, born after Saturn entered Pisces, has the out-of-sign water version. [D]
- Astrology King lists Saturn–Pluto square dates across decades. Its own natal text doesn't discuss the generation. [S]
- **Writing note:** say plainly that much of Lu's age group shares the square. What's personal is that it lands on the 2nd/10th axis and is tied into the Moon–Mars–Saturn cluster.

**Orb:** 6°51' is moderate to wide for a square. Astrolog still ranks it the 4th strongest aspect in the chart (power 27.45), because both planets are strong in this chart (Saturn 4th, Pluto 1st in planet power). [S: source-lu-astrolog.txt]

**Sourcing flag:** Cafe Astrology has no Saturn-square-Pluto page I could find (its Saturn–Pluto URL 404s; only the conjunction and sextile pages exist). kerykeion.net blocks fetching. natalecho.com is a newer site of unknown standing. **Moderate sourcing: Astrology King plus natalecho, with Cafe's conjunction and sextile pages for background.**

## 3. Sun sextile Uranus · Pisces Sun ⚹ Capricorn Uranus · 5°15' · 2nd/12th

**Core meaning [S]**
- Originality, inventiveness and progressive ideas that come naturally, without needing to rebel. They question rules that don't make sense but don't feel those rules as a threat to their freedom (Cafe).
- Unpretentious and honest; hard to shock; relate to all kinds of people; good to confide unusual ideas to (Cafe).
- Very creative and good at finding new ways of doing things; drawn to science, technology and unusual subjects; like standing up for the underdog (Astrology King).
- A sextile works when used: Cafe says sextile people can call on these qualities when needed, while trine people have them built in. [S]
- **Difficulty (mild):** routines are hard to keep; interests jump around. Astrology King says the restlessness tends to lead to discoveries more than to problems. Cafe lists no real difficulty. [S]

**What the signs add**
- Uranus in Capricorn is generational (1988–1996) and sits conjunct Neptune 2°29' (exact three times in 1993, about every 168 years). [S: digest §7, source-lu-astrolog.txt]
- Pisces Sun with Capricorn Uranus [D]: water–earth sextile. The originality is applied and practical: changing structures and systems more than shocking people. It joins Pisces imagination to Capricorn's interest in what can actually be built.
- Uranus is the modern ruler of Aquarius, Lu's rising sign, so this sextile links the Sun to the modern chart ruler. [D, using digest §3 rulers]
- **Also notable [D]:** the Sun is sextile Neptune 2°45', tighter than this one, and Uranus and Neptune are conjunct. So the Sun really sextiles the whole Uranus–Neptune pair in the 12th: originality (Uranus) and imagination (Neptune) arriving together. Sun sextile Neptune is in the Astrolog list (#8) and is presumably covered in the other batch.

**What the houses add [D]**
- 2nd (values, earning) sextile 12th (hidden life, solitude, behind the scenes). Original ideas may come from private time, dreams or work done out of sight, and can be turned into income or a value system. The 12th-house placement suggests the originality is quieter than the textbook Uranus picture.
- Uranus in the 12th is also 6°38' from the Ascendant (see #11), so it partly colours how you come across.

**In-sign / out-of-sign:** in-sign (Pisces–Capricorn, two signs apart). [D]

**Generational:** no for the aspect, since the Sun reaches it every year. Uranus's sign is generational. [D]

**Orb:** 5°15'. Fine by the Sun's wider allowance, but at or past the 4–5° sextile orb in Wikipedia's ranges, so medium to soft. Applying: the Sun is moving toward the exact 60°. [D]

## 4. Jupiter trine Saturn · Scorpio Jupiter (R) △ Pisces Saturn · 9°34' · 10th/2nd (wide)

**Core meaning [S]**
- Balance between optimism and realism: they see both the upside and the risks of a plan, and avoid both over-hope and pessimism (Cafe).
- They learn from experience, have good judgment and business sense, and are patient and persistent. Steady, planned progress over quick success; a confidence that life rewards work (Cafe).
- Sustained ambition, constancy of purpose, organizing and leadership ability; respected for real achievement, not luck. Will put off rewards for long-term goals (Astrology King).
- **Difficulty:** frustration when there's no visible chance to advance (Astrology King). Cafe frames the aspect as producing quiet "pillars of society": ambitious, honourable and fairly conventional. [S]

**What the signs add**
- **Rulership link [S + D]:** Saturn is in Pisces, Jupiter's traditional sign, so Jupiter traditionally disposes Saturn. The trine and the rulership point the same way: Saturn's discipline answers to Jupiter's faith.
- Jupiter in Scorpio (depth, money luck, intensity; Astrostyle) trine Saturn in Pisces (compassion, boundaries, fear of dissolving; Astrostyle). [S]
- Water trine [D]: faith and discipline both work through feeling. A plausible reading is patient emotional and spiritual endurance: hope that's realistic about pain, and discipline in service of something felt rather than imposed. Saturn is also traditional ruler of the Aquarius Ascendant (the chart ruler), so this links Jupiter to the traditional chart ruler.

**What the houses add [D]**
- 10th (career, reputation) trine 2nd (money, self-worth). This is the classic "career and money" pairing for a Jupiter–Saturn trine: steady work (Saturn in the 2nd: slow, careful building of security and worth) supported by Jupiter's growth and opportunities in public life. Same 2nd–10th trine geometry as #1.

**In-sign / out-of-sign:** in-sign (Scorpio–Pisces). [D]

**Generational: partly [S + D].**
- Cafe says Jupiter–Saturn aspects come in a roughly 20-year cycle and are shared by large groups of people. [S]
- Ephemeris check [D]: this water-sign trine was exact on 29 Apr 1994 and 29 Aug 1994 (an earlier air-sign pass was exact 13 Oct 1993). It was within 10° from about March to October 1994. At Lu's birth it was **applying**, coming into orb: Jupiter retrograde and Saturn direct were closing the gap. People born a few months either side share it.

**Orb:** 9°34' is wide, at the outer edge of the 5–10° trine range. Astrolog gives it 1.65 power (16th of 20). Write it as a background steadiness, not a headline. Its applying status is a small point in its favour for astrologers who weigh that. [D]

## 8. Mercury conjunct Mars · Aquarius Mercury ☌ Pisces Mars · 8°30' · 1st/2nd (out-of-sign)
