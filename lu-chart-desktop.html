Glossary

℞Retrograde

Seen from Earth, a planet sometimes appears to slow down, stop and drift backwards through the zodiac for a few weeks or months. It never really reverses. The effect comes from Earth and the other planet moving at different speeds, like a car you overtake seeming to slide backwards.

In a birth chart, a planet that was retrograde when you were born is usually read as working inward. Its themes get reflected on, revisited and made your own before they show outwardly.
