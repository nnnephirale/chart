# Design notes
Page: `lu-chart-desktop.html` (desktop rebuild of `lu-chart-ios.html`; the originals are untouched).
References: 34_DESIGNSYSTEMS/03_resurf (motion), 02_perfolios (section dot, blur-in), 01_superhuman (shader plate + glass border). Precedence: house style, with the references' motion applied.

## Project rules
- Type: Instrument Serif for titles (modest, 19–27px; page name 40px), IBM Plex Sans Light for reading, IBM Plex Mono Light for labels, data and controls. Glyphs: Noto Sans Symbols, always text presentation (U+FE0E).
- Colour is data only: sign element (water, fire, earth, air) and aspect type. Element colours are clear, not muted (2026-10-02: "too dull"): water #1E98AE, fire #E2733A, earth #A08A3C, air #5C8BE0; the zodiac ring tints at 18% (36% when focused). Aspect colours stay muted. Chrome stays monochrome on a #FCFCFC → #F4F4F4 ground.
- One focus model: hovering or tapping any planet, sign, house or aspect, on the wheel or in any list, lights the same thing everywhere: the wheel, the centre label, the inspector and the aspect grid. Clicking the wheel locks what it is showing (2026-10-02): hover freezes, and the card reads "· Locked". Clicking another part of the wheel moves the lock; clicking anywhere outside the round chart (or Esc) releases it. The wheel toolbar toggles keep the lock; panel rows move it.
- The inspector's lozenges (All · Planets · Signs · Houses · Aspects) are the wheel's mode (2026-10-02). All is the default: every element answers to hover on its own hit area, everything stays at full strength, and its list is a directory of planets, signs and houses. Only the lozenges change the mode; locking an element (on the wheel or from a panel row) never switches it. In a specific mode the whole wheel answers only to that kind: Planets, anywhere within 12° of a planet around the ring; Signs and Houses, anywhere in that slice from the centre out, shaded as a wedge; Aspects, the nearest line inside the circle (within 10 units, respecting the type filter). Switching the lozenge lets go of the current focus. The selected mode's layer stays at full strength and the rest steps back (2026-10-02): sign ring and house numbers/cusps at 35%, planets at 30% (focused ones stay full), aspect lines at 14% outside Aspects mode and 80% inside it.
- The side panel does one job at a time: with nothing in focus it shows the mode's list (with a one-line hint); while the wheel is hovered, or something is locked, the list is replaced by a full-height detail card (planet: placement and every aspect; sign: planets and house cusps; house: planets and cusp sign; aspect: strength and both planets). Hovering lists or rows only lights the wheel; it never swaps the panel under the cursor.
- Text over symbols: the Glyphs/Names toggle labels signs, houses (with a one-word theme) and planets in words; the inspector lists everything as text.
- Conjunctions are drawn as an arc between the fanned glyphs, not as a chord (a 1° chord is invisible).
- Motion: segmented controls glide on the Resurf lozenge spring (.45s, bounce .18); the section dot glides on the house ease-out (380ms, no overshoot, no swell); sections fade up on scroll (opacity + 8px, 500ms); inspector views crossfade on opacity. No blur anywhere on desktop (2026-10-02: "restraint is very important"; blur was also the main lag source). The glass rim is translucent white without backdrop blur. All of it stops under reduced motion.
- Performance: the plate shader renders at 0.75× CSS px, ~30fps, 3 noise octaves (the other 3 had zero weight), and only reallocates its buffer after a resize settles, so the rail push doesn't thrash it.
- The shader plate frames the hero only, in a water palette (Lu's chart is 49% water), behind glass-rimmed white cards. It pauses offscreen.

## Screen notes
- No transits by request ("aspects, not transits", 2026-10-02).
- No top bar on the chart (mobile's "Lu · Natal chart" bar removed 2026-10-02); the page header carries the title. The phone's rest-of-chart sheet has its own auto-hiding bar (2026-10-19).
- Desktop (≥961px) only, 2026-10-02: no top bar. The chart plate fills the screen and the wheel scales to fit the browser height. Sections live in a collapsed icon rail docked on the right (moved from the left, 2026-10-02) that opens on hover (80 → 236px) and pushes the page across; icons stay pinned to the outer edge and labels open inward. The wheel's legend sits under its toolbar, at the top of the card, inset 16px so it lines up with the Glyphs/Names text. Its element key (Water · Fire · Earth · Air) shows only in Signs mode or while a sign is locked; the aspect key is always there. (The phone layout is below.)
- Inspector text shares one 20px inset with the tabs and list rows.
- Aspect titles read as one phrase, "Moon Trine Jupiter", in the wheel centre and the inspector; the subheading carries the orb and strength (2026-10-02). The centre name is measured and shrunk to fit the disc.
- Aspect rows in a planet's card use the ranked list's system: planet name over a strength track in the aspect colour, then the coloured aspect glyph and type name (ink-2, darkening on hover), then orb.
- Planet glyphs are centred by their ink (measured on a canvas), not their em box, so each sits in the middle of its halo; the hover scale runs from the halo centre.
- A focused house sets its number and name in 500 weight and darkens its two cusp lines. In the centre label for a house, the planets line takes the strong style and the theme · cusp line the quiet one.
- Every named thing in the panel is its own link (2026-10-02): a planet row's sign and house, the planet glyphs in sign and house rows, the planets inside aspect rows, and the sign and house in a card's position line. The innermost link wins; clicking one locks that item. Sign and house cards also list the aspects their planets make. Clicks on the open card's own text keep the lock. Rows take keyboard focus; Enter or Space locks.
- Panel swaps never overlap: the outgoing list or card leaves at once and the incoming one fades in (150ms), so text never ghosts. The same goes for switching lozenge lists.
- Mode tabs (All · Placements · Signs · Houses · Aspects), every viewport (2026-10-02): a row of words on one unbroken grey line (ink-5, 1px); the active word goes ink and carries a 1.5px black underline that glides between tabs (350ms house ease, no spring). Text-width underlines, 22px gaps, first word on the 20px inset. On phones the row runs edge to edge and scrolls sideways; the ends fade (28px mask) only where more tabs sit off-screen, and the chosen tab scrolls to the centre. The aspect-type filters (in the panel's Aspects list and the Ranked aspects card) use the same underline row, named: coloured glyph + type + muted count. Any underline row that overflows, at any width, scrolls sideways with the end fades and centres the chosen option. Glyphs/Names stays a segmented pill.
- The element key is removed from layout when hidden (it no longer leaves an empty legend line on phones) and fades in when shown.
- Sign and house rows name their planets (glyph + name tags, left-aligned, wrapping), in the lists and the cards.
- Every ℞ in the side panel is a button that opens the Retrograde explainer: a centred modal over a 40% black, 4px-blur backdrop (white card, 20px radius, layered shadow), 240ms rise-in and 150ms fade-out, closed by ×, the backdrop or Esc. It lists this chart's retrograde planets and notes the North Node is routinely ℞. It never touches the lock.
- Sign lists (All and Signs tabs, 2026-10-02) run from most planets to fewest. Ties go clockwise round the wheel (backwards through the zodiac), starting from the sign listed just before the tied group; a tie at the very top starts from Aries. Lu's order (with Lilith in Aries, 2026-10-18): Pisces, Scorpio, Aries, Capricorn, Virgo, Aquarius, then Sagittarius, Libra, Leo, Cancer, Gemini, Taurus.
- The Planets tab is named Placements (2026-10-02), because it also lists the North Node, Chiron and the two angles. The Ascendant and Midheaven sit at the end of that list as their own focus kind ("Angle"): hovering or locking one darkens its axis line and ASC/MC label on the wheel and opens a card with its sign, house and descriptors. In Placements mode the wheel picks the nearest planet or angle; in All mode the ASC and MC labels are the hit areas.
- Detail cards carry descriptors (2026-10-02): under the position line sit the tag (`f-note`), then a quiet general line and an "in your chart" paragraph (`f-desc`, sans 13.5px). The copy lives in `CONTENT.cards` (planets and angles by name, signs by name with their tag); planet tags stay in `CONTENT.pnotes`. House cards (tag + descriptors) and aspect cards (tag under the strength bar, then the aspect type's general line and the pair's reading) follow the same pattern (2026-10-02).
- Section labels and rail names (2026-10-02): Configurations is now Clusters and Prompts is now Questions; section titles name what the section holds (e.g. Houses / Twelve houses, five with planets).
- Placements section (2026-10-17, after Costar's birthchart breakdown): one block per house, 1st to 12th, empty houses skipped. Each block reads left to right: sign (element dot + name), the planets in that sign, then the house number (with its one-word theme on wide screens) on the right. A house with planets in two signs stacks two sign rows under one number. Planets and the Ascendant/Midheaven sit in order round the zodiac from the house cusp. Hover shows "Show on chart"; notes hide below 960px.
- Black Moon Lilith (mean, 26°59' Aries, 3rd house; added 2026-10-18 from the user's own figure, birth 11 Mar 1994 4:10am). A point like the Nodes: it is the last entry in `PLANETS`, shown on the wheel, in the lists and in the 3rd-house block. It has no aspects in the source, so the aspect grid skips it. The ⚸ glyph is the standard Lilith mark. Its card copy is draft, awaiting the user's read.
- Phone layout (≤960px, 2026-10-19; desktop unchanged). The chart is a page of its own that never scrolls: the shader plate fills the screen with the name and a "Read your chart" pill on it, then one white card holding the toolbar, the wheel (a square at full width, the first thing to give way on a short screen), the info area and the mode tabs along the bottom by the thumb. The legend and wheel note are hidden.
  - Info area, under the wheel: the focused item's name (glyph + serif) with ‹ › on the right, its position line, its tag, then its "in your chart" text, at least three lines and more on taller screens, fading out under a "More" button. With nothing locked it reads "Your chart": Sun, Moon and Rising signs, the chart signatures and a one-line hint. ‹ › walk the current mode's list in order (and start it from rest). Tapping the text or More opens the full card.
  - Zoom: locking anything glides the wheel's camera to it (viewBox tween, 700ms ease-out expo, interruptible, instant under reduced motion). Planets and angles frame glyph, tick and degree; signs and houses their slice of the rings; aspects both ends. The frame stays inside the chart's bounds and the clipped edges fade over 12px. Letting go glides back. After Lemniscate's mobile spread guidance.
  - Sheets: the full card (the inspector, reused) and the index rise from the bottom (86% height, 500ms house ease, 40% black + 4px blur scrim, × to close; tap the scrim or Esc too). One sheet at a time; closed sheets are fully off-screen with no shadow peeking.
  - Index ("Everything in your chart"): placements, signs, houses and aspects in one list, with section jumps at the top. It opens by tapping the name (marked ▾) or by long-pressing anything on the wheel (480ms, a light vibration where supported); it scrolls to and marks the pressed or locked item. Tapping a row locks it and closes the sheet.
  - The rest of the chart (every section below the chart) is a full-screen sheet that slides up from "Read your chart" and back down with ×, landing you on the wheel as you left it. Its bar (title + ×) is frosted and auto-hides on scroll down. Story chips and "Show on chart" work inside it; anything pointing at the chart closes it and locks that item.
  - Phone cards drop the "· Locked" wording.
